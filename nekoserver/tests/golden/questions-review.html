NekoServer 0.50.0: the questions setup asks

One block for each question, in the order setup shows them. The light box is what the setup window shows: the question, one line under it, and its buttons. The dark box is the same question in the terminal, with one line on how to answer there. "Before" is the old text, where the question existed. The names (Jellyfin, drive 2, an address) are examples.

What to install

THE SETUP WINDOW · a switch for each, then Continue and the review

Photos and phone backup - Immich

Store and browse your photos and back them up from your phone.

Movies and shows - Jellyfin

Watch your films and shows on any device, and ask for new ones to be found and added.

Music - Navidrome

Play your music collection from your phone or browser, and have new music found and added.

Personal files - Nextcloud

Access your files from your devices.

Also find and download new ones

Finds and downloads films and shows you are entitled to, and adds them to your library. You choose where it searches later.

Also find and download new ones

Finds and downloads music you are entitled to, and adds them to your library. You choose where it searches later.

A TV connected to this PC

Play games from your gaming PC and watch YouTube on the TV this PC is plugged into: Steam Big Picture with Remote Play, a YouTube shortcut and Bluetooth for a controller. Leave it off if this PC has no TV.

Send alerts to my phone

A message on your phone, through the free ntfy app, when a backup or an app fails.

Warn me if the server stops checking in

This server checks in with an outside service you choose (Healthchecks.io, say). When the check-ins stop, after a power cut for example, that service warns you.

Add a local AI assistant

Ask Neko, in the dashboard: it explains what the dashboard shows and what to do about a problem, and can suggest a fix that runs only when you approve it. Its model runs on this PC; setup shows how much memory each model needs before you choose one.

My media and backup drives are in a network storage box (NAS)

For a NAS that offers iSCSI disks (a WD My Cloud EX, Synology, QNAP...) or shares folders over NFS. The SSD for the apps stays on this PC. Setup asks for the NAS's address, sees which of the two it offers, and asks which disks or folders to use; it never formats one, and tries a shared folder out before using it. Experimental: tried in a test lab, not yet on many real NAS boxes (SUPPORT.md).
